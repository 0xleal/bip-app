## Solution Section

### Headline
**Your Work. Your Voice. Automated.**

### How It Works (3-step visual flow)

1. **Connect Your Tools**
   - Link GitHub (commits, PRs, reviews)
   - Link Notion (pages, databases, docs)
   - Add manual notes for quick thoughts
   - *Visual: Integration logos connecting to central hub*

2. **AI Learns Your Voice**
   - Paste 3-5 of your existing tweets
   - AI analyzes your style, tone, and patterns
   - Every suggestion matches how you actually write
   - *Visual: Tweet examples transforming into style guide*

3. **Generate & Share**
   - Click generate—get 3-5 diverse content options
   - Pick what resonates, edit if you want
   - Copy and post to Twitter/X
   - *Visual: Multiple tweet options with one-click copy*

## FAQ Section

### Common Questions

**Q: Does this replace my creativity?**
A: No! BIP generates starting points based on your real work. You pick what resonates and edit to add your spin. Think of it as a creative assistant, not a replacement.

**Q: Will my posts sound robotic?**
A: Not at all. The tone of voice analysis ensures every suggestion matches your writing style. That's the whole point.

**Q: What if I don't use Notion?**
A: You can still use BIP with just GitHub and manual notes. But Notion integration unlocks richer context for better content suggestions.

**Q: How does rate limiting work?**
A: To keep costs predictable, you get 3 generations per day and 10 per week on the free tier. This encourages thoughtful sharing over spam.

**Q: Can I customize what activities get tracked?**
A: Yes, you can control which repos and Notion pages are synced. (Note: Build this feature if it doesn't exist)

**Q: Do I need to be a developer?**
A: While built for developers, anyone who uses GitHub and Notion can benefit. If you're creating and documenting work, BIP helps you share it.

## Design & Copy Principles

### Voice & Tone
- **Conversational but credible** — Like talking to a developer friend who gets it
- **Honest about limitations** — Don't oversell AI magic
- **Empathetic to pain points** — Acknowledge the real struggle of building in public
- **Action-oriented** — Every section should move toward signup

### Visual Style
- **Clean, minimal dashboard screenshots** — Show the interface is modern and easy
- **Developer-friendly colors** — Dark mode option, code-inspired palette
- **Icons for features** — Visual scanning helps busy developers
- **Real content examples** — Show actual generated tweets (with permission)

### Conversion Optimization
- **Multiple CTAs** — Don't rely on one button
- **Social proof placement** — After problem and solution sections
- **Frictionless signup** — OAuth = 2 clicks to start
- **Show value immediately** — Generate first content in onboarding
